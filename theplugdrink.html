      Stipulations Needed for Funding

        How these are worked out
        • Factor rate = buy rate + points. One point adds 0.01.
        • Buy rate — 18 month: 1.29 at 7 points or less, 1.31 above. 12 month: 1.21 at 7 or less, 1.23 above.
        • Payments — 74 weekly on the 18 month, 50 on the 12. Weekly payment is the payback divided by that count, rounded up to the cent, matching the portal.
        • Origination fee 3%, charged by the lender and deducted from the funded amount.
        • Caps — Fora's 12 month tops out at $182,000 and comes off the portal entirely above that. Its 18 month runs to $250,000, which is also where the four stips drop off. PEAC and Wall are approved to $250,000.
        • PEAC — buy rate is fixed per term and does not move with points. Payments are months × 52/12 (52, 78, 87) and the weekly is rounded, not rounded up. Fee is 2.5% weekly, 3% monthly, 4% on 100% prepayment — and 100% prepayment caps commission at 7 points and the term at 18 months with no change in buy rate.
        • Wall Street Funding — one point moves the factor 0.01, same as the others. Their printed sheet carries a 0.04 spread over buy, so it is a 4-point quote even though its POS column reads 2; the page reproduces it exactly at 4 points. Fee is 1.5% plus a flat $35. 51 level weekly payments and a final one trued up. Moving the points shifts every early repayment tier by 0.01.
        • Early payoff figures are the lender's own prepayment schedule, read off the portal. They are only shown at 7 and 10 points because those are the two the portal has given us.
