Line of Credit

APR product · $0 fee · 100% prepay · to $200,000
The line is an APR-amortizing product, not a factor rate: interest accrues on the outstanding principal and the payment is a level amortization over the term. No draw fee, no origination fee, and it prepays at 100% — pay it off any time and only interest accrued to that date is owed. The draw below is the slider amount, capped at $200,000.

Notes

  • Rates: OnDeck quoted max sell = buy on every row, so the sheet models no commission. Payback = amount × rate; weekly payment = payback ÷ payments.
  • Interest forgiveness: on early payoff 100% forgives all remaining interest; 25% forgives a quarter of it. The higher rate at each term is the 100% option.
  • Fees: 2.5% origination on 6–18 month term loans, 4% on 24 months, $0 on the line of credit. Fees are deducted from funding.
  • Line of credit: APR 41.5% at 12 months, 40.7% at 18, 40% at 24. Weekly view uses APR ÷ 52 over term × 52 ÷ 12 payments; monthly view uses APR ÷ 12 over the term in months (OnDeck's portal runs the line monthly).
