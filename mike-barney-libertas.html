Notes

  • Sell rate = buy rate + points ÷ 100. Payback = loan amount × sell rate. Weekly count = term × 52 ÷ 12, rounded (18 mo → 78, 12 mo → 52, 17 mo → 74), which is how Libertas' own $6,371.80, $8,682.70 and $6,621.63 come out. The weekly payment rounds up to the cent.
  • Commission = loan amount × points ÷ 100, paid at funding. If the merchant uses a prepay, commission is capped at 5 points and anything above 5 is returned (sold at 8 on $350,000: $28,000 paid, $17,500 kept, $10,500 returned). The 2% origination fee is the lender's charge to the merchant and is separate from commission.
  • Libertas only printed the prepay ladder for Offer 1. It's applied to Offers 2 and 3 here as well; the payoff by month doesn't depend on the term, so only the savings columns change. Confirm with Libertas before quoting their prepay.
  • APR is on the Libertas approval (52.40% / 56.81% / 52.93% at 8 points) but deliberately not shown here.
  • Soft offer: subject to Libertas' review of the stips above and the merchant interview, and may change.
