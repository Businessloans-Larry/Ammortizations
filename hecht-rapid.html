Notes

  • Factor rate is the total-cost multiple: payback = funding amount × factor. There is no origination or closing cost on the term loan — closing cost is $0.00.
  • Points move the factor, not the funding. Dropping from 8 to 6 points cuts 0.02 off the factor — on $250,000 that is $5,000 less payback for the client and $5,000 less commission.
  • Final payment may differ by a few cents so the payments sum exactly to the payback amount.
  • No amortization schedule is shown — the term loan is a fixed-fee product, and any payoff figure other than the amendment above comes from the lender's own schedule once contracts are requested.
  • A $55,000 line of credit is also approved on this file and can be taken alongside or instead of the term loan — quoted separately.
