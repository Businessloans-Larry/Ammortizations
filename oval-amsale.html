Notes

  • Factor rate is the total-cost multiple: payback = funding amount × factor. There is no separate origination or closing cost on this file.
  • Points move the factor, not the funding. Dropping from 8 to 5 points cuts 0.03 off the factor — on $250,000 that is $7,500 less payback for the client.
  • Final payment may differ by a few cents so the payments sum exactly to the payback amount.
  • Amortization / early-payoff schedule is not shown here — it will be built from the lender's own schedule once contracts are requested.
  • A line of credit option is also available on this file; pricing on request.
  • Terms above reflect the lender's proposal and are subject to final underwriting and the stipulations listed.
