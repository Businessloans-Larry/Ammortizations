      • Factor / sell rate is the total-cost multiple: payback = amount × rate. None of the three calculates interest, so there is no amortization schedule on any of them.
      • Fees are where these differ most. Rapid has no closing or origination cost. Fora deducts an origination fee at funding. Byzfunder deducts an origination fee plus a flat $995 processing fee. The same headline amount does not put the same money in the account.
      • Payment counts differ by lender — Rapid uses 78 weekly payments on an 18-month, Fora uses 74, Byzfunder uses 52 over ~13 months. That alone moves the weekly payment even at an identical payback.
      • Early payoff works three different ways. Rapid discounts 20% of the fixed fee inside a window that scales with the term. Fora settles the whole balance at a month-band rate, and its first five months hold the same rate. Byzfunder settles at a day-band rate that climbs every 30 days.
      • Commission is not comparable at equal points. Fora's upsell runs to 15%, Rapid's to 8, Byzfunder's to 5 — the same client cost can pay very differently.
      • Byzfunder's term is fixed at 52 weeks here. Their portal allows 16–52 weeks and a shorter term prices at a lower rate, but that rate curve was not in the approval — ask for a re-quote rather than backing into it.
      • Only Fora's 18-month was quoted; other terms exist in their portal. Their prepayment schedule covers months 1–9.
      • All three are subject to final underwriting and the stipulations listed. Byzfunder and Fora also require a merchant interview.
